Who We Are

We know that good design means good business.

We are a UK-based company specialising in the manufacturing of Aluminium Foil Containers. All our production takes place in our factory in England, UK.

Aluminium foil containers offer a range of advantages as food packaging — lightweight, durable, non-porous, compatible with various food types, sanitary, odour and moisture resistant, with excellent thermal conductivity, reusability, and recyclability.

These properties make them ideal for households, restaurants, cafes, and catering businesses, ensuring food freshness, safety, and efficient processing while minimising environmental impact.
